## How Users Install as App Icon

### On Desktop (Chrome/Edge)
1. Visit https://MandaStrong01-mandas-ahja1.bolt.host
2. Look for the install icon (➕) in the address bar
3. Click "Install MandaStrong Studio"
4. The app will install as a standalone application
5. Opens directly to Page 1 (Home Screen)

### On Mobile (iOS Safari)
1. Visit https://MandaStrong01-mandas-ahja1.bolt.host
2. Tap the Share button (⬆️)
3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
4. Tap "Add" to confirm
5. The MandaStrong Studio icon appears on home screen
6. Tap to open - goes directly to Page 1

### On Mobile (Android Chrome)
1. Visit https://MandaStrong01-mandas-ahja1.bolt.host
2. Tap the menu (⋮) in the top right
3. Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"
4. Tap "Install" to confirm
5. The icon appears on home screen
6. Tap to open - goes directly to Page 1
