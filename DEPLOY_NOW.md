# 🚀 Deploy MandaStrong Studio NOW - Simple 3-Step Guide

Your app is **100% production-ready**. All files are built and ready to go live.

---

## Option 1: Netlify (EASIEST - 2 Minutes)

### Step 1: Sign Up
Go to https://netlify.com and create a free account (or log in)

### Step 2: Deploy
1. Click "Add new site" → "Deploy manually"
2. **Drag and drop your entire `/dist` folder** onto the page
3. Done! Your app is live instantly

Your URL will be: `https://random-name-12345.netlify.app`

### Step 3: Custom Domain (Optional)
- Go to Site settings → Domain management
- Add your custom domain (like `mandastrong.com`)

**That's it! Your app is LIVE.**

---

## Option 2: Vercel (Also Easy - 2 Minutes)

### Step 1: Sign Up
Go to https://vercel.com and create a free account

### Step 2: Deploy
1. Click "Add New" → "Project"
2. Click "Deploy" tab
3. **Drag and drop your `/dist` folder**
4. Click "Deploy"

Your URL will be: `https://your-app.vercel.app`

---

## Option 3: Bolt.new (Current Platform)

You're already here! To make it live:

### Step 1: Check Deploy Button
Look for the "Deploy" or "Share" button in Bolt.new interface

### Step 2: Connect Host
- Follow the prompts to connect to Netlify or Vercel
- Or export project and upload manually
